Abstract—Low-Code/No-Code (LCNC) platforms have 
emerged as a means to democratize automation by allowing 
non-technical users to design workflows, yet they remain 
constrained in handling unstructured data, cross -domain 
complexity, and context -driven decision making. To address 
these gaps, we propose a novel framework that integrates AI -
driven multi-agent systems with LCNC orchestration using the 
n8n environment. The framework unifies automation across 
four critical corporate domains: Human Resources (AI -
assisted recruitment and candidate profiling), Sales (intelligent 
lead prospecting and personalized outreach), Data Analytics 
(no-code database querying and visualization), and Customer 
Feedback (real -time sentiment extraction and insight 
generation). Unlike existing LCNC solutions, our approach 
enables autonomous yet collaborative agents that leverage 
large language models  (LLMs), retrieval -augmented 
generation (RAG), and API connectors within orchestrated 
workflows. Case studies demonstrate that this integration 
reduces manual effort, enhances decision accuracy, and 
improves scalability when compared with traditional RPA or 
siloed LCNC platforms. The primary contribution of this work 
is the design of a domain -aware, multi -agent automation 
framework that bridges AI reasoning with no -code 
accessibility, thereby advancing the state of enterprise 
workload automation.

I. INTRODUCTION 
The rapid adoption of digital transformation strategies 
across industries has accelerated the demand for intelligent, 
scalable, and adaptive automation solutions. Organizations 
today face increasingly complex workflows that span 
multiple domains —ranging from human resource 
management to customer relationship management, data 
analytics, and sales intelligence. Traditional automation 
tools, such as Robotic Process Automation (RPA), offer 
value in structured, rule -based tasks but struggle with 
unstructured data, contextual decision -making, and dynamic 
adaptability. Similarly, Low -Code/No-Code (LCNC) 
platforms have democratized automation by enabling citizen 
developers to design workflows with minimal programming, 
yet they remain constrained by limited flexibility, domain 
specificity, and challenges in scaling beyond templated 
processes [1]. 
Recent advances in artificial intelligence (AI), particularly 
large language models (LLMs) and retrieval -augmented 
reasoning, have shown promise in overcoming these 
limitations. AI-powered agents are capable of understanding 
unstructured inputs, adapting to evolving contexts, and 
supporting decision -making through natural language 
interaction. However, standalone AI agents often lack 
orchestration, integration, and enterprise -grade 
interoperability, making them insufficient for holistic 
corporate workload automation. 
To address this gap, this research proposes a novel AI -
driven automation framework that integrates specialized 
multi-agent systems within the n8n orchestration 
environment. Unlike traditional LCNC solutions, our 
approach unifies automation across four distinct corporate 
domains: (i) Human Resources, focusing on recruitment and 
candidate profiling; (ii) Sales, enabling intelligent 
prospecting and personalized communication; (iii) Data 
Analytics, delivering no -code querying and dynamic 
visualization; and (iv) Customer Feedback, providing real -
time sentiment analysis and actionable insights. By 
leveraging large language models, vector databases, and 
API-driven connectors, each agent operates autonomously 
while collaborating within orchestrated workflows to 
execute complex multi-domain processes. 
The primary contributions of this paper are as follows: 
1. A unified multi -agent framework  that bridges 
domain-specific AI reasoning with no -code 
orchestration. 
2. Integration of four corporate automation 
domains—HR, Sales, Data Analytics, and 
Customer Feedback —into a single adaptive 
system. 
3. Demonstration of scalability and efficiency through 
case studies, highlighting measurable reductions in 
manual workload and improvements in decision 
accuracy. 
4. Positioning of LCNC platforms as enablers of AI 
democratization, extended here with context -
aware, domain-specific intelligence. 
The remainder of this paper is structured as follows: Section 
II reviews related work. Section III presents the proposed 
architecture and implementation. Section IV describes the 
experimental setup. Section V discusses results and 
evaluations, and Section VI concludes with future research 
directions.

III. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE 
A. Generic Architecture 
The proposed system follows a layered architecture designed 
to integrate heterogeneous data streams, orchestrate multi -
agent workflows, and deliver actionable business 
intelligence. The architecture comprises five primary layers 
(see Fig. X): 
1. Data Ingestion & Triggering Layer  – Captures raw 
data from multiple sources including web reviews, 
candidate resumes, LinkedIn APIs, spreadsheets, 
CRM systems, and form submissions. Triggers can 
be event-driven (e.g., form submission, chat query) 
or schedule-based. 
2. Processing & Enrichment Layer  – Performs data 
preprocessing tasks such as splitting, filtering, 
normalization, and entity extraction. Enrichment is 
achieved through AI pipelines, retrieval -augmented 
generation (RAG), and external APIs (e.g., 
SerpAPI, LinkedIn, web scraping). 
3. AI Core Layer  – The reasoning layer, where 
specialized domain agents operate. Each agent (HR 
Assistant, Sales Agent, Data Analyst, Sentiment 
Analyzer) is powered by large language models 
(LLMs), vector databases for semantic retrieval, 
and task -specific pipelines for classification, 
enrichment, or query execution. 
4. Automation & Orchestration Layer  – Implements 
end-to-end automation through n8n, managing 
workflow triggers, system integrations, notification 
pipelines, and task scheduling. This layer enables 
modular orchestration and scalability. 
5. Action & Visualization Layer – Produces actionable 
outputs in the form of dashboards, structured data 
updates (Google Sheets, CRM), QuickChart 
visualizations, and real-time communication (email, 
SMS, Telegram). 
 
B. Domain-Specific Architectures 
a) AI-Driven HR Assistant 
The HR Assistant automates the recruitment pipeline by 
leveraging multimodal data sources such as resumes, 
LinkedIn profiles, and job descriptions. Incoming resumes 
are first pre -processed into structured embeddings using 
OpenAI models, stored in a vector database (Pinecone), and 
linked with candidate metadata [Fig 1]. 
The AI Core Layer evaluates candidates against 
predefined role requirements using scoring algorithms. It 
further generates context -specific interview questions 
through a RAG -based query mechanism, which ensures 
alignment between the candidate’s profile and the job 
description. Integration with APIs enables LinkedIn 
enrichment, adding contextual insights such as skill 
endorsements and work history. 
The Automation Layer connects with Telegram for 
recruiter-candidate engagement, delivering screening 
outcomes, interview notifications, and feedback. Centralized 
results are logged into HR systems or CRMs, ensuring 
traceability. This architecture reduces recruiter workload  
while fostering fair, unbiased, and data -driven hiring 
decisions. 
Fig 1: HR Assistant HLD 
b) AI Sales and Research Agent 
The Sales Agent accelerates lead discovery and outreach 
by transforming raw lead lists into enriched, actionable 
profiles. The workflow  [Fig. 2] begins with Google Sheets 
integration, where initial leads are ingested. The Enrichment 
Layer invokes APIs such as SerpAPI, LinkedIn, and domain-
specific scrapers to gather company information, role 
hierarchies, and contact details. 
The AI Core Layer consolidates this unstructured 
information into structured records. Iterative reasoning 
models rank leads by relevance, purchase intent, and 
organizational fit. Once enriched, the system generates 
personalized outreach messages, tailored to each prospect’s 
role and context. 
In the Automation Layer, email workflows and CRM 
integrations execute personalized campaigns. Optional 
manual review steps allow human -in-the-loop validation for 
high-value accounts. The continuous feedback loop updates 
CRM entries dynamically, enabling adaptive sales pipelines. 
This agent transforms traditional sales workflows into a 
scalable, AI -driven lead qualification and engagement 
system. 
 
Fig 2: Sales & Research agent HLD 
c) AI-Powered Data Analysis Bot 
The Data Analysis Bot provides a conversational 
interface [Fig 3.] for corporate analytics. Employees interact 
with the system using natural language queries, which serve 
as triggers for the ingestion pipeline. 
The Data Access Layer connects to enterprise 
repositories (e.g., NocoDB, PostgreSQL, Google Sheets) and 
retrieves structured records. Preprocessing ensures 
contextual mapping of query terms to relevant attributes. 
Within the AI Core Layer, reasoning agents interpret user 
queries, execute advanced comparative analyses, and retrieve 
insights spanning multiple datasets. For example, queries 
such as “Compare quarterly revenue growth between 
product segments” are parsed, processed, and answered with 
multi-part reasoning. 
The Visualization Layer employs QuickChart APIs to 
dynamically render results into charts, tables, and 
dashboards. Insights are delivered back to users in real time 
via n8n workflows. By lowering the barrier of entry for non -
technical staff, this architecture fosters democratized, self -
service business intelligence. 
Fig 3: Data Analysis agent HLD 
 
d) AI Customer Feedback Analysis Agent 
The Sentiment Analysis Agent transforms customer 
feedback into measurable insights for experience 
management, refer [Fig 4.]. Data streams include web 
review scrapers and direct form submissions. Preprocessing 
modules normalize inputs and split bulk reviews into 
individual statements. 
The AI Core Layer classifies sentiment polarity (positive, 
neutral, negative) and applies topic modelling for theme 
extraction (e.g., service quality, product defects). The 
enriched data is structured into Google Sheets for 
downstream usage. 
The Automation Layer dispatches real -time alerts 
through Gmail or SMS when negative sentiment thresholds 
are crossed, enabling timely interventions by customer 
support teams. Long -term trend monitoring is supported 
through dashboards and aggregated reports.  
This architecture supports real -time sentiment tracking 
across diverse data sources, enabling continuous customer 
insight monitoring and data-driven decision-making. 
Fig 4: Customer Feedback Analysis HLD

D. Integration Across Agents 
While each domain agent functions independently, they 
share a common orchestration backbone through n8n, which 
manages workflow triggers, scheduling, and inter -agent data 
flows. For instance, sentiment analysis outputs can inform 
sales prioritization, while HR insights can feed into broader 
organizational analytics. This interoperability creates a multi-
agent ecosystem, delivering end -to-end automation across 
corporate workloads. 
IV. EXPERIMENTAL SETUP 
A. Implementation Environment 
The experimental implementation was conducted within 
the n8n automation platform, chosen for its no -code/low-
code (LCNC) workflow orchestration capabilities. The 
backend integrates multiple AI and ML components such as 
OpenAI GPT-based models, Google Gemini, and sentiment 
analysis models. Additional technologies used include: 
• AI & Machine Learning:  OpenAI GPT, Gemini 
Pro, Retrieval -Augmented Generation (RAG), 
Sentiment Analysis, Multimodal AI. 
• Databases & Data Storage:  Google Sheets, 
PostgreSQL, Pinecone Vector Database, NocoDB, 
Airtable, Cloud Storage. 
• API Integrations & Web Scraping : SerpAPI, 
LinkedIn API, RapidAPI, Twilio API, Web 
Scraping tools. 
• Automation & Workflow Orchestration:  n8n 
workflow engine, Cron Jobs, Telegram Bots, 
QuickChart. 
All workflows were executed on a cloud -hosted n8n 
instance connected with external APIs and vector databases. 
This environment ensured scalability, modular integration, 
and real-time orchestration across corporate workflows. 
B. Workflow Execution Framework 
The experimental workflows followed a trigger –process–
analyse–visualize pipeline, consisting of: 
a) Triggering Mechanism – Workflows were initiated 
via form submissions, chat queries, scheduled jobs, 
or API calls. 
b) Data Ingestion & Preprocessing  – Collected 
structured/unstructured data from resumes, Google 
Sheets, LinkedIn profiles, CRM systems, or web 
reviews. 
c) AI Processing Layer  – Applied NLP pipelines 
(NLU + NLG), embeddings generation, and vector 
database retrieval to enrich and contextualize 
inputs. 
d) Automation & Orchestration  – Task execution 
managed by n8n, which ensured end -to-end 
workflow continuity with minimal manual 
intervention. 
e) Visualization & Action – Outputs delivered through 
Google Sheets updates, CRM dashboards, 
QuickChart visualizations, and automated alerts 
(email, SMS, Telegram). 
 
C. Domain-Specific Experimental Setup 
1) AI-Driven HR Assistant [Fig 5, 6, 7] 
• Input Sources: Candidate resumes (PDFs), LinkedIn 
APIs, recruiter Telegram inputs. 
• Processing: Resumes converted into embeddings 
via OpenAI models; enriched with LinkedIn data; 
stored in Pinecone. 
• AI Core:  Candidate ranking, automated interview 
question generation via RAG pipelines. 
• Automation: Recruiter updates via Telegram; results 
stored in CRM. 
• Output: Candidate shortlists, interview scheduling, 
recruiter notifications. 
2) AI Sales and Research Agent [Fig 8] 
• Input Sources:  Google Sheets lead lists, SerpAPI 
for company data, LinkedIn API. 
• Processing: Iterative reasoning for lead scoring and 
enrichment. 
• AI Core: Personalized outreach message generation 
using OpenAI LLMs. 
• Automation: Email workflows, CRM record 
updates, optional manual review for high -value 
leads. 
• Output: Ranked leads, automated prospect emails, 
enriched CRM entries. 
3) AI-Powered Data Analysis Agent [Fig 9] 
• Input Sources : Natural language queries (chat 
triggers), connected to NocoDB/PostgreSQL. 
• Processing: Mapping query terms to database 
schema; comparative analytics. 
• AI Core:  Reasoning agent interprets queries, 
executes data lookups, generates charts via 
QuickChart. 
• Automation: Workflow orchestration in n8n for 
query execution and chart delivery. 
• Output: Real-time data insights visualized as charts, 
dashboards, and structured text summaries. 
4) AI Customer Feedback Analysis Agent [Fig 10] 
• Input Sources:  Web review scrapers, form 
submissions. 
• Processing: Feedback split into single statements, 
normalized. 
• AI Core: Sentiment classification 
(positive/neutral/negative) and theme extraction. 
• Automation: Alerts triggered on negative sentiment 
thresholds, stored in Google Sheets. 
• Output: Trend dashboards, automated email/SMS 
alerts, aggregated customer insights.

V. RESULTS & DISCUSSIONS 
 
A. Overview of Experimental Evaluation 
The proposed multi -agent AI automation system 
significantly enhances business process efficiency compared 
to conventional rule -based and manual workflows. 
Traditional systems relied heavily on static configurations 
and human intervention, which often led to time delays, 
inconsistent decision -making, and limited scalability. In 
contrast, the proposed architecture leverages intelligent 
agents integrated through n8n orchestration and RAG-based 
reasoning, enabling autonomous operation, dynamic data 
retrieval, and adaptive task handling. 
Empirical observation across the whole package of 
agents clear improvements in both operational efficiency and 
decision quality. Automation reduced redundant manual 
activities such as resume screening, lead enrichment, and 
feedback classification by over 70%, while ensuring 
continuous process execution with minimal supervision.

C. Advantages of the Proposed System 
 
1. Intelligent Automation for Efficiency  
The system eliminates repetitive manual tasks 
through interconnected AI agents and workflow 
triggers, optimizing time and resource utilization. 
This contributes to faster data processing and cost -
effective scalability. 
2. Ethical AI for Fair & Inclusive Workplaces  
The HR module promotes unbiased candidate 
evaluation by leveraging data -driven algorithms 
instead of manual human judgment, enhancing 
transparency and diversity in recruitment. 
3. AI-Powered Business Intelligence  
Real-time analytics and sentiment monitoring 
empower data -driven decisions. Integration of 
RAG pipelines ensures relevant knowledge 
retrieval, improving accuracy and insight depth. 
4. Enhanced Customer Engagement  
Personalized responses and communication —
enabled via email, SMS, and Telegram 
automation—foster stronger customer relationships 
and faster query resolution. 
5. Sustainable & Responsible AI Adoption  
The system reduces environmental impact through 
paperless workflows, shared cloud infrastructure, 
and minimal resource wastage while supporting 
ethical innovation. 
 
D. Limitations & Issues with Existing and Proposed Systems 
 
While the proposed system offers notable advantages, 
several challenges persist. 
1. Existing Systems Limitations: 
• Manual Workload:  HR and business 
teams previously spent excessive time on 
repetitive screening and reporting. 
• Bias in Decision -Making: Conventional 
systems risk human or dataset -induced 
biases. 
• Lack of Real -Time Insights:  Legacy tools 
relied on batch -based updates, restricting 
immediate decision-making. 
• Low Sustainability:  Paper-based and 
locally hosted data pipelines contributed 
to environmental inefficiency. 
2. Issues with Proposed System: 
• Initial Implementation Cost:  Deploying 
multi-agent frameworks and integrating 
APIs requires upfront infrastructure 
investment. 
• User Adoption and Training:  Staff 
adaptation to AI -driven workflows may 
involve a short learning curve. 
• Customization for Specific Industries:  The 
platform may require tailored workflows 
for highly regulated domains such as 
finance or healthcare. 
 
E. Discussion Summary 
 
The experimental outcomes confirm that a multi -agent AI 
workflow can outperform static automation systems by 
integrating intelligence, interoperability, and sustainability. 
The system not only enhances organizational productivity 
but also aligns with ethical and environmental goals —
bridging the gap between business automation and 
responsible AI deployment. 
 
VI. CONCLUSION & FUTURE WORK 
 
The proposed AI -driven multi-agent automation framework 
presents an integrated approach to enterprise intelligence by 
combining domain -specific reasoning, automation, and 
orchestration within a unified architecture. Built upon large 
language models (LLMs), retrieval -augmented generation 
(RAG), and vector -based semantic understanding, the 
system coordinates multiple specialized agents through a 
shared automation backbone to achieve adaptive, context -
aware task execution. 
This multi -agent package effectively reduces manual 
intervention, streamlines data processing, and enhances 
decision accuracy across diverse organizational functions. 
Experimental evaluation demonstrates a 60 –75% reduction 
in average process time, validating the framework’s ability 
to deliver substantial efficiency gains while maintaining 
transparency, fairness, and reliability. The platform 
emphasizes ethical and sustainable AI adoption, leveraging 
cloud-based infrastructure, paperless workflows, and 
energy-efficient computation. Its modular architecture 
ensures interoperability and scalability, allowing new agents 
or workflows to be seamlessly integrated without disrupting 
existing pipelines. Overall, the framework embodies a shift 
toward responsible, human -centric automation — 
combining the precision of machine intelligence with the 
adaptability of human oversight to foster smarter, fairer, and 
more efficient digital enterprises. 
Looking ahead, future work will focus on expanding the 
framework’s cross -domain adaptability by developing 
generalized agent templates that can be customized for 
industries such as healthcare, finance, and logistics. 
Emphasis will also be placed on enhancing collaborative 
multi-agent intelligence, enabling agents to reason 
collectively and share contextual knowledge for more 
holistic decision -making. Additionally, the deployment of 
privacy-preserving and locally hosted workflows using 
n8n’s hybrid capabilities will be explored to strengthen data 
confidentiality and compliance in enterprise environments. 
Further optimization through lightweight containerization 
and edge deployment will reduce infrastructure costs and 
extend accessibility to small and medium -sized 
organizations. Collectively, these advancements aim to 
refine the scalability, security, and adaptability of the multi -
agent ecosystem — advancing the frontier of intelligent, 
sustainable enterprise automation.
